Nigeria's Geological Wealth

Nigerian Minerals

Showcasing the rich mineral diversity of Nigeria — from the gold deposits of Zamfara to the tin mines of Jos Plateau and the strategic minerals driving the green energy revolution.

Gold (Au)

Native Element

Found extensively in Zamfara, Osun, Kebbi, and Niger States. Nigeria's gold belt spans the northwest with both primary lode and alluvial deposits.

19.3 g/cm³ 2.5-3 Mohs
Zamfara, Osun, Kebbi, Niger

Native Silver (Ag)

Native Element

Occurs as a byproduct in Nigeria's lead-zinc deposits, particularly in the Benue Trough. Often associated with galena mineralization.

10.5 g/cm³ 2.5-3 Mohs
Benue, Ebonyi, Cross River

Lithium (Li)

Spodumene / Lepidolite

Critical mineral for EV batteries. Nigeria has significant deposits in Nasarawa, Kwara, Ekiti, and Kaduna States, positioning it as a key player in the green energy transition.

0.53 g/cm³ 6-7 Mohs
Nasarawa, Kwara, Ekiti, Kaduna

Monazite

(Ce,La,Nd,Th)PO₄

Rare earth phosphate mineral found in Nigeria's coastal beach sands. Contains cerium, lanthanum, neodymium - critical for electronics and renewable energy.

5.0-5.3 g/cm³ 5-5.5 Mohs
Lagos, Ogun, Ondo (Coastal)

Cassiterite (SnO₂)

Tin Oxide

Nigeria was once the world's 6th largest tin producer. The Jos Plateau hosts extensive deposits that shaped Nigeria's mining history.

7.0 g/cm³ 6-7 Mohs
Jos Plateau, Bauchi, Kano

Iron Ore (Fe)

Hematite / Magnetite

Abundant in Itakpe (Kogi), Agbaja, and Ajabanoko deposits. Nigeria has over 3 billion tonnes of iron ore reserves for steel production.

5.2 g/cm³ 5.5-6.5 Mohs
Kogi, Enugu, Kwara

Zircon (ZrSiO₄)

Zirconium Silicate

Found in Nigeria's coastal heavy mineral sands alongside monazite. Used in ceramics, nuclear reactors, and as a gemstone.

4.6-4.7 g/cm³ 7.5 Mohs
Lagos, Ogun, Ondo (Beach Sands)

Columbite (FeNb₂O₆)

Iron Niobium Oxide

Nigeria is a major source of columbite (niobium ore). Essential for aerospace alloys, superconductors, and electronics.

5.2 g/cm³ 6 Mohs
Jos Plateau, Nasarawa, Cross River

Tantalite (FeTa₂O₆)

Iron Tantalum Oxide

Critical for capacitors in smartphones and electronics. Found with columbite in the "Coltan" deposits of Nigeria's tin belt.

8.0 g/cm³ 6-6.5 Mohs
Plateau, Nasarawa, Kogi

Galena (PbS)

Lead Sulfide

Nigeria's Benue Trough hosts significant lead-zinc deposits. The Abakaliki area is particularly known for this mineralization.

7.6 g/cm³ 2.5 Mohs
Ebonyi, Benue, Cross River

Wolframite

(Fe,Mn)WO₄

Tungsten ore found in Nigeria's Younger Granite province. Critical for high-temperature applications and cutting tools.

7.5 g/cm³ 4-4.5 Mohs
Plateau, Kaduna, Bauchi

Rutile (TiO₂)

Titanium Dioxide

Found in beach sands and placer deposits. Used for titanium metal, pigments, and aerospace applications.

4.25 g/cm³ 6-6.5 Mohs
Lagos, Cross River, Akwa Ibom

Ilmenite (FeTiO₃)

Iron Titanium Oxide

Major source of titanium in Nigeria's heavy mineral sands. Essential for aerospace and pigment industries.

4.7 g/cm³ 5-6 Mohs
Coastal States, Plateau

Barite (BaSO₄)

Barium Sulfate

Critical for oil drilling operations. Nigeria has significant deposits supporting its petroleum industry.

4.5 g/cm³ 3-3.5 Mohs
Taraba, Benue, Cross River

Tourmaline

Complex Borosilicate

Colorful gemstone found in Nigeria's pegmatite regions. Oyo State produces quality specimens for the gem market.

3.0-3.2 g/cm³ 7-7.5 Mohs
Oyo, Kwara, Nasarawa

Sapphire

Corundum (Al₂O₃)

Precious gemstone found in Nigeria's gem-bearing gravels. The country produces both blue and fancy-colored varieties.

4.0 g/cm³ 9 Mohs
Kaduna, Plateau, Taraba

Nigeria is endowed with over 44 different mineral types across its 36 states. This gallery showcases key minerals with exploration and economic significance.
