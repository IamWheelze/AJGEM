About Me

8+ Years Experience

Professional Geophysicist & GIS Specialist

I am a passionate geoscientist with a strong foundation in applied geophysics and advanced expertise in remote sensing and geoinformatics, dedicated to sustainable mineral exploration and environmental stewardship. My operations are primarily based in Nigeria, where I have worked across multiple states for mineral exploration projects — from the gold belts of Zamfara to the tin-bearing Plateau province and the strategic mineral corridors of Nasarawa, Kogi, and beyond.
